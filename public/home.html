How it works

Three steps, and not an app in sight.

First

Create your event

Sign in and name it. You get your own address at yourname.kamemories.com and a printable QR card to set on the tables.

Then

Guests share photos

They scan the code and post from their own phones. No accounts, no downloads. Every photo lands privately, waiting for your eye.

Finally

You curate the gallery

Approve the keepers, feature the best on your event's home page, and let the rest go. Nothing is public until you say so.

The whole night, seen through every eye that was there.
